How to read the factors

  • Availability: enter only the estimated impact of confirmed or well-sourced availability news.
  • Rest: favor the team with more rest; back-to-backs and 3-in-4-night stretches should be recorded separately in the source data.
  • Travel: capture distance, time zones, and direction. Do not assume distance alone creates an edge.
  • Net rating: a compact prior-strength input. Update it from a consistent statistics source.
